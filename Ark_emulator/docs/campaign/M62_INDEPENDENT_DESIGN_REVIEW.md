Minimum implementable contract:

Sampling+rule+effects+counter+reschedule are one atomic transaction. Origin/field UID remains explicit; source=None is reserved and must not resolve to battle or a virtual actor. No-source damage requests supply fixed_amount700 and true damage, environmental/ignore-SP flags and target, with target-side after hooks and health allocation stats. Ordinary source-based paths remain untouched. The pure membership operation should supply effective selection states (availability, side/motion/category, combat predicate), apply after geometry and re-check live eligibility before each packet.

Two cells with identical time need stable declared ordering and independent state, not necessarily separate RNG streams. Disk restore must preserve insertion/initialization order and pending generation guards. A source field removed/scene completed during callback cannot reschedule a stale task. No-opt must compare every event, snapshot and checkpoint apart from explicit identity fields.
